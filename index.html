Helplines, Support & IT Act Legal Rights

Immediate 24/7 national helplines, college counseling contacts, and Indian legal provisions protecting students against cybercrimes.

🚨 Immediate Emergency Contacts

📞

National Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930

Operated 24/7 by the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C), Ministry of Home Affairs. Official reporting portal: cybercrime.gov.in.

Call 1930 Now
🧠

Tele-MANAS Mental Health: 14416

National 24/7 free mental health counseling helpline by Ministry of Health & Family Welfare for stress, trauma, anxiety, and depression caused by bullying.

Call 14416
🎓

National Anti-Ragging Cell: 1800-180-5522

UGC 24x7 Toll-Free Anti-Ragging Helpline. Covers both physical ragging and digital harassment targeting college freshers and seniors.

Call 1800-180-5522

⚖️ Legal Provisions (Information Technology Act & IPC)

Section 66E - IT Act 2000

Bodily Privacy Violation

Capturing, transmitting, or publishing photographs or videos of a person's private parts without consent. Punishable with up to 3 years imprisonment or fine up to ₹2 Lakh.

Section 66D - IT Act 2000

Cheating by Personation

Creating fake profiles, impersonating faculty, seniors, or classmates on social media for harassment, deception, or reputational damage. Up to 3 years imprisonment.

Section 67 - IT Act 2000

Publishing Obscene Content

Transmitting sexually explicit or obscene material in electronic form. Strictly penalized under cyber criminal jurisdiction.

Section 354D - IPC / BNS

Cyberstalking

Monitoring the internet, email, or any other form of electronic communication of a person despite disinterest. Cognizable and non-bailable offense on repeat.

Section 507 - IPC / BNS

Anonymous Criminal Intimidation

Sending death threats, blackmail, or intimidation while concealing identity via burner accounts or pseudonyms. Imprisonment up to 2 years.

How to Preserve Proof
